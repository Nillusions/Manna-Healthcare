GDA Staffing: Elderly Care Attendants & Caretakers

General Duty Assistants (GDAs), also called patient attendants or caretakers, help with the everyday, non-clinical tasks that keep a patient comfortable, safe and cared for. Many families choose an attendant for elderly care at home.

  • General Duty Assistants (GDAs)
  • Patient attendants and caretakers
  • Elderly care attendants
  • Daily patient assistance
  • 24-hour attendants
  • Mobility assistance
  • Personal hygiene support

GDA support is not nursing care. GDAs and attendants provide personal and daily-living assistance. Clinical tasks such as medication administration, wound care or critical-care monitoring are handled by our nursing service.
